# 📑 Description

Looks to be the same exact time period as [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md), only the last two generations of kids that were born are all queers, every single one of them, no straights and a few bisexual people. Now that the older generation is dying out, the newer gay generation is seemingly left to die out. They have zero intention of figuring out a solution to people no longer reproducing and it doesn't seem like they care. A few big corporation first invested trillions into finding the cause, but eventually they realized if they found some sort of "cure" all the homosexuals made it clear they have no intentions of taking it.

# 📝 Notes

> Instead of having kids for the sake of the survival of humanity, the people here just started to paint everything rainbow colored and now they kind of do anything they want. A lot of people no longer work jobs and are fully off grid, the ones that do work only work in government or critical jobs like at power companies and hospitals. Most of the people here are nice and friendly, the night time scene here can get pretty wild at times.
