# 📑 Description

The entire planet in this dimension is the same as the one from "adventure time" in [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md), the time period however seems to be somewhere near the start of the show. There are tons of monsters, creatures, and even magic, all throughout the world. All of the same people and creatures from the show also exist here, some of which are very threatening.

# 📝 Notes

> Theres a ton of dungeons with battles, puzzles, and even loot you can find, its just as epic as the cartoon makes it out to be. Can be very fun at times, others times extremely challenging, there are many real dangers in this world despite the cartoony aspect and logic to a lot of things.
