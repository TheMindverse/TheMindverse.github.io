# 📋 Cosmic Entry

- **ID:** MV-Z02
- **Name:**  The Machinescape
- **Classification:** [None](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)
- **Difficulty Level:** [Advanced](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)
- **Risk Level:** [Challenging](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

# 📑 Description

All of our life forms work together as a massive interconnected hivemind, unknowingly powering the universe by solving internal algorithms necessary for the universe to expand. This is done in physical form on a cellular level such as brain neurons working together, and on a conscious level with our consciousness acting as a neuron in a way to the universe. It is not known of the machinescape created the universe as a way to power it, or if its a byproduct of consciousness forming for the first time as life came into existence.

The only way you can access the machinescape when bound to an organic body is through psychedelics, as they let you see the internal algorithms that make up your consciousnesses. Connecting to [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md) while in this altered mental state allows you to connection regions of your brain in normally impossible ways, harnessing all possible combinations of signals from the cosmos.
