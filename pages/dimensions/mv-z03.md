# 📋 Cosmic Entry

- **ID:** MV-Z03
- **Name:**  The Dreamscape
- **Classification:** [Chimera](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

- **Risk Level:** [Safe](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

# 📑 Description

The dreamscape is an alternative method used to view dimensions in the multiverse, only it is very limited and does not allow for true consciousness navigation like the mindverse does. The visions and experiences you have while in the dreamscape work on a subconscious level, you can overwrite your natural REM sleep signals with ones from the cosmos. Although this method is largely one way and you have little control of where you go, other than some basic influence of your memories as they are being processed while you sleep.

# 📝 Notes

> Can be very unpredictable and hard to control, learning to lucid dream can help, but its always chaotic and all over the place.
