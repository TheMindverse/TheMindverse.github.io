# 📑 Description

The exact same world, people, places, and creatures, as the lord of the rings, seemingly a couple decades after the destruction of the one ring. Strangely, this world has an [ethereal classification](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md) when one might assume spectral, due to the nature of the fantasy and magic elements. Its mostly safe as long as you don't wander off into unknown caves or fortresses looking for trouble.

# 📝 Notes

> The most breathtaking dimension I've explored. Personally I love to hang out in the shire, the most peaceful and happy place in middle earth. I even have my own little hobbit home where I sit and read and tend to a small farm. I only wish I could visit the elves and stay in their city.
