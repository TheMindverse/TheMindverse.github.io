# 📑 Description

Many different variations of this world exist for different emotions, but this is the closest one I could find in the same time and technological period as [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md). In this world, everyone is guaranteed to have a soulmate and and instant every generation that is born, no one is single, a lot of the world and public buildings are focused around love and hearts and even classical mythology themes, like cupid.

# 📝 Notes

> Im not sure how the soulmate thing works, could possibly be hardcoded in their DNA as it happens every generation cycle. Or maybe everyone is just similar enough to each other where common interests, hobbies, and careers, are all relatable instead of everyone being unique.
