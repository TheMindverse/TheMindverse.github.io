- **Risk Level:** [Safe](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

# 📑 Description

A world where the only land animal are cats, as a result they have gotten exceptionally good at swimming and hunting for fish. The cats are very friendly, perhaps due to possibly tens of thousands of years of not having any natural predators, and they all seem to friends with each other. Every cat eventually ends up here to live out the remainder of their cosmic life.

# 📝 Notes

> A very cute and fun world to spend time in if you like cats, if you had cats at one point in your life you can find them here too. I found both of mine in here and I often come back to visit them to see how they're doing.
