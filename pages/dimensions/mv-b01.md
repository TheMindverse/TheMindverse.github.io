# 📑 Description

A very similar world and time period that'd you see on the walking dead in [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md), only without the characters from the show and exact places. The world itself, the virus, time period, are all follow the same logic and rules as the show. The people here are only focused on food, shelter, and survival. 

# 📝 Notes

> One of my favorite dimensions to hang out in, no responsibilities, can loot anything you want and go anywhere, scavenge for food and build shelter, its everything you could want in a zombie apocalypse world. Building up defenses on a home and wandering into down for weapons and supplies are the most fun, but between that there's not much to do for entertainment. You can also straight up just kill people here like its no big deal.
