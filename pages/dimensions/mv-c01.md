# 📋 Cosmic Entry

- **Name:** Hoxxes IV
- **Classification:** [Spectral](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

- **Risk Level:** [Hazardous](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md)

# 📑 Description

A very similar world and planet from "deep rock galatic" in [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md), only there is no space rig, dwarves, or glyphids. The only living creatures that have been observed here are plant life, and stationary creatures such as cave leeches, spitball infectors, korlok tyrants, and more.

# 📝 Notes

> Very difficult to travel here, the only times Ive ever been able to visit required being on dissociatives which dramatically decreases your navigational control in both [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md) and in dimensions themselves. It feels like you are constantly floating or flying in a single direction without ever stopping.
