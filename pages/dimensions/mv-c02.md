# 📑 Description

An exact replica of the universe and planets of "astroneer" from [earth prime](pages/dimensions/mv-a00.md), infinitely generating caves and terrain with unique biomes to explore. Lots of cave layers to explore, minerals, and plant life most of which are hostile and act in self defense.

# 📝 Notes

> Just like ⁠[hoxxes iv](pages/dimensions/mv-c01.md) it is pretty hard to navigate to here. Dissociatives aren't required to visit here unlike ⁠⁠hoxxes, I've done it once or twice while meditating and listening to the astroneer soundtrack at the same time.
