# 📑 Description

The mirror is an interdimensional event that can occur as a result of spending too much time in [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md) without traveling anywhere.
In combination with [deadwalking](pages/guides/advancedguide.md), when looking into a mirror and staring at your own face it slowly starts to twist and become contorted. Your face melts and forms facial expressions that seem impossible, like you're looking at another person in control. If you start replicating the faces in the mirror, which can happen accidentally if you don't pay attention, the faces will take full control. You will no longer be only seeing them in the mirror, you will start doing it outside of looking into the mirror, and you will act psychotic. You can snap out of it, but the mood it puts you in can make it hard to want to stop because of how natural it feels.

# 📝 Notes

> These guys and their forces are extremely fun, the mood it creates is incredibly psychotic. A sense of pure euphoric insanity.
