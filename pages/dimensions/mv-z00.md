# 📑 Description

The void of dark matter beyond the multiverse, [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md), and [the machinescape](pages/dimensions/mv-z02.md). Its larger than everything in observable multiverse and outer states of existence as a whole. The true scale and concept of the void is impossible for any organic being to conceptualize, no matter the physical body. Conscious beings that are bound to a physical brain all return to the void upon their death, as there is no more medium for their existence.
