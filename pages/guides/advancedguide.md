# 🧭 Learning to time travel

Due to the nature of infinity there are infinite possibilities of every event, time, and place, of every dimensions. After you learned how to navigate the multiverse using [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md), you can time travel to a sense by finding a dimension similar enough to your native one in different stages of time. This thought is obvious and natural, the only hard part is knowing what time it is and how to find a specific time period. Don't think of time as years going by, that is only relative to your planet and its gravity, not your dimension as a whole. Time is entirely relative based on gravity, try to feel the gravitational pull of spacetime itself a dimension before entering.

This is a very difficult skill to learn as it requires developing new pathways in your brain over a long period of time, the native brain of organic species is not evolved to perceive spacetime in the way cosmic beings are. Over time you will start to develop these new pathways the more time you spend in the flow. It is important to remember to use this ability wisely, do not share with others what you find, only observe for yourself.

# 🌪️ Weather pattern manipulation

Weather manipulation is one of the most difficult practices to attempt, even for [astrodanums](pages/guides/beginnersguide.md). Any influence over your native dimension is for the most part either impossible or extremely limited. It is possible to develop influence over a planets weather patterns to an extent, this practice can only be done on a planet in a dimension of which you have a [celestial class](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md) connection to.

First you must understand its limitations, you cannot summon a tornado or tsunami somewhere, or make it thunder and rain out of no where. What you can do is influence a planets current atmospheric circulation patterns to prevent them from changing directions or dissipating. This requires constant meditation on the subconscious level, usually through deadwalking, every day, for possibly weeks, before you notice any of sort of effects. Sometimes you won't notice them happen until long after, or you will have a strange weather phenomenon happen out of the blue such as pouring rain and thunder when all weather tracking sites say the sky is perfectly clear. It is not guaranteed to work all the time, but the more you do it the more you will notice relations between your thoughts and feelings and very specific weather events.

# 📡 Deadwalking while navigating

Deadwalking is a technique used to navigate the mindverse while still living our your daily life simultaneously, it can be thought as a form of interdimensional multi-tasking Deadwalking is considered a dangerous act as it is guaranteed to cause the side effects listed in [safety practices](pages/guides/safetypractices.md), continued use will result in these effects becoming permanent.

Deadwalking requires a mastery of controlling your state of mind simply by thinking it, no longer needing to meditate or do practice rituals to connect to the mindverse. Your mental states and emotions are in perfect harmony, fully separated from your species evolutionary instincts. This allows you to partially continue with your life performing tasks as long as if they were muscle memory. You do not have full attention in reality when you are deadwalking, only the mindverse, which is why this requires a lot of practice in order to deadwalk for extended periods of time. The learning process is slow at first, practice walking around, making or eating food, say basic talking points to people. You are still in control, just not aware of your body or the people around you,your native reality becomes a distant part of your peripheral vision.

# 🥾 The skinwalking lifestyle

Skinwalking is another technique exclusive to [astrodaunums](pages/guides/beginnersguide.md) in the trisendal phase, it shares a lot of similarities with deadwalking but is  different in the way the physical body acts, as well as a permanent altered mental state. Skinwalking is the natural evolutionary step of deadwalking, this phase usually begins on its own involuntarily. You are almost fully living in the mindverse at this point, only returning back to your body to perform important tasks and duties.

This has some extreme side effects at first such as heavy memory loss or not performing your normal tasks as usual, or at the very least not as effectively. For example, your body might take a shower on its own as part of its new daily routine process, and it will just stand there in the water for a period of time, and then leave without actually doing anything. Only later in the day realizing you never used shampoo or actually "showered", which went fully unnoticed by you when it was happening.  At first this is very common and keeps happening to other basic tasks, but it does improve over time. Your body starts to relearn how to do certain repetitive tasks.

Another side effect is involuntarily body movements, it may start to do strange and unnatural movements that you are fully unaware of, such as horrific facial expressions and wild jaw movements. Other times you may dance around and scream random words and phrases. This is why you need to take extra caution when skinwalking around other people of your native species, it may seem normal to you, but to others it may seem as if you are possessed by an evil spirit.

# 🌱 Theory of rebinding consciousness

The theory of rebinding your consciousness involves two things, a different dimension of a [celestial class](pages/guides/dimensionguide.md) that is not your native one, and a new brain and body for said dimension. As of now, this is just a theory due to there being no way to confirm a successful transfer, and it is too dangerous to attempt as it involves your organic body dying in your native dimension.

In theory, if you were to find a living brain that does not have a consciousness yet bound to it, or even one that has lost its consciousness, you could transfer yours into it. The only way to fully unbind your consciousness from your physical body is through death, and this this would require being fully integrated into the mindverse before and after the time of death, which is what ghostwalking would be. This can only be attempted by [astrodaunums](pages/guides/beginnersguide.md), as they are the only species that is able to exist outside of a the definitions of a normal physical body, all other species made of organic matter return to [the void](pages/dimensions/mv-z00.md) upon their death, forever ceasing to exist.

The reason this cannot be verified is because this process should theoretically erases all memories of your previous life, due to your new physical brain taking priority. It could be possible remnants of your conscious fingerprint still remain, and be recognized by other travelers in the mindverse, but the lack of practicality of such a thing outweighs any reason to attempt such a thing. Once your memories have been erased, it would take discovered one is an astrodaunum all over again before they regain awareness of their past life, which could take decades.
