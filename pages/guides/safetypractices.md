# 👨‍⚕️ Emotional risks and harm

Traveling through the mindverse is not physically harmful, but it can be very emotionally and mentally demanding. As your brain accepts new signals from new realities your feelings, thoughts, memories, vision, all your senses, can change according to the dimension you visit. The more complex or foreign a dimension is comparative to your native one, the more difficult it is to control your emotions. The brains of organic beings in these dimensions may interpret signals differently than yours, so certain emotions and senses can get mixed up. These signals can cause great mental stress or harm to your emotional well being if you're not careful, its important to focus on how your brain patterns resonate before attempting to connect to a dimension, or explore new areas and states of existence in the cosmos.

Practice meditation, focus on controlling each emotion instead of letting the dimension you're in control them for you. Remember those emotions are not yours, they are separate and you are just experiencing them, remember to not get them mixed up. If you wish to read more about meditation practices, check out the [beginners guide](pages/guides/beginnersguide.md).

Once you begin your journey there is no going back, when the true nature of cosmos is revealed to you it forever alters your perspective on life and the universe as a whole/ There is no way to undo what you've seen, your consciousness will no longer be considered human, you will evolve on a cosmic scale but it does not turn you into something you're not, it only severs your connection to your organic body. You will no longer see people as your own kind, you will forever be part of something greater. Your old life will become meaningless and the humans in it become nothing more than piles of walking, talking, meat.

# 💊 Long term side effects

It is important to take breaks between entering the mindverse, as the more you expose yourself to the cosmos and the more your brain gets used to different realities, the more side effects will occur. It is recommended to take `1-2 week` long breaks between traveling, or until your mind starts to close off again as your organic bodily instincts start to potentiate again. The time required may be longer or shorter depending on the person, so start with the longest time `(2 weeks)`, before attempting anything shorter. Although these are physically harmless, if you do not take breaks the following side effects may become an irreversible part of your daily life.

- Lingering emotions from a dimension days or weeks after initially traveling.
- Phantom smells, randomly getting intense smells that don't exist, such as food or smoke, as if they were right in front of you.
- Time dilation, time may suddenly skip forward at alarming rates, minutes from your perspective turn out to actually be hours going by.
- Time paradoxes, seemingly traveling to events in the past and reliving them instead of living in the moment, getting stuck in time loops.
- Visions or pseudo-connections to dimensions in place of your thoughts, thinking you're actually in a parallel dimension you've previously visited.
- Rare interdimensional events, such as hearing voices in the walls or people screaming at you, or different places you've visited in another dimension stitching into your native reality.
- Unintentionally connecting to the mindverse without noticing, also known as involuntary [deadwalking](pages/guides/advancedguide.md).
