# 🪔 Meditation practices

Meditation can help you master controlling your mindset and emotions, which is necessary if you wish to connect to the mindverse. Your mindset and setting are the two most important aspects of forming a connection to [the flow](pages/dimensions/mv-z04.md). When practicing meditation rituals, it is important to be alone and fully closed off from the societal hivemind, the longer you remain in social isolation the clearer your thoughts and desires become. Being around your native species can cloud your judgement and negatively impact your intentions.

Practice controlling your breathing, control your emotions and make them as neutral as possible, practice feeling your brains inner hum. Try to visualize the algorithmic patterns that power your brain, see and feel beyond what your organic body feeds your brain. Slowly you will enter a trace like state, your breathing will become noticeably smoother like the air is made of thick warm honey, your whole body will feel slightly numb and start to tingle as if it were vibrating on a molecular level.

You will start to see connections to things you never have before, impossible things will start making sense as your connection to your mortal being starts to become severed.

# 💤️ Exploring the dreamscape

The dreamscrape is a very limited version of the mindverse, which offers little control over where you travel to or what you experience. It relies more on your subconscious feelings and desires, as opposed to direct thoughts. Using the skills you learned when practicing meditation and controlling mental states, try to visualize pathways to other dimensions while dreaming, focus on feeling the hum of your brain as it enters REM sleep. Learning to feel and recognize the different algorithmic patterns your brain enters in different states, is vital to mastering everything about the mindverse, including the dreamscape.

It is easier to do this asleep as this is when your imagination is at its peak, as the technique of rerouting your physical body into your emotions, and controlling your emotions in return, is a similar process to rerouting your brain to accept the signals from the cosmos instead of your body. Learn to see by just feeling the new signals your brain is receiving, not by your eyes. Check out [the dreamscape](pages/dimensions/mv-z03.md) for more info on its existence.
