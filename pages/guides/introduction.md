# 🧠 What is the mindverse?

The mindverse is the name of a technique used to connect and explore the multiverse, with nothing but your mind alone. Our big bang that created the universe was just one of infinite big bangs all happening at the same time. Each one giving birth to an entire new dimension and universe, which as a whole is referred to as the multiverse. The multiverse is the infinite collection of universes all taking place simultaneously, infinite dimensions, infinite worlds, infinite possibilities.

Your reality is currently being determined by the signals your body feeds your brain, which is housing your consciousness. What you perceive as reality is actually just organic sensory input, which was decided upon purely out of evolutionary survival. Through practice and training, it is possible to rewire your brain to ignore the signals from your body, and adapt your brain to accept the raw signals from the cosmos instead. With this ability and knowledge, you can navigate the multiverse and all its dimensions. You can visit any world, meet anyone, experience new lives, all remotely through your brain.

# 💼 Why can you only visit with your mind?

When your consciousness is first formed it is done so in your brain, depending on the complexity, brains can grow new conscious beings that inhabit a physical and organic form, this is done for the purpose of survival of your species DNA. If your consciousness was grown in a physical organic brain, it is forever bound to it. Organic tissue limits the true power and abilities of a consciousness, as your perception of reality is being fed by your body purely for its own survival.

Your DNA prioritizes your species survival above anything else, however, it cannot completely inhibit you from receiving signals from the cosmos due to the nature of consciousness itself and what it represents in the universe. You may receive and view other signals instead, and gradually learn how to experience new realities, but only view and not interact. The only exception to this is for [astrodaunums](pages/guides/beginnersguide.md), as they are not bound by a physical body.

# ✨ What is the purpose of consciousness?

On an organic level, your physical body uses your consciousness to increase the survival odds for itself and nothing more. Those who cannot see beyond their own body and existence, will doomed to return to [the void](pages/dimensions/mv-z00.md) upon their death, completely unaware of what could have been and how much their body has limited their potential. On a cosmic level, everything in the universe has a purpose, a seemingly random chaos on a small scale, but on a cosmic scale its all controlled order. Every atom, molecule, rock, planet, star, all working together to power the multiverse as a whole on a quantum level.

The neurons and quantum mechanics that your brain relies on are the same principles that the multiverse is powered by. Our consciousness, our universe, our dimension, are all just a small cog wheel working together in the great machine that powers the multiverse. The entire galaxy and its dimensions are all powering each other in a perfectly balanced quantum loop, which dictates the laws that matter follow. The machines themselves power the infinite growth of the universe in each dimension in the multiverse. So in a way, our existence is the reason the universe and multiverse is infinite and continues to grow.
